AI features consume usage that's billed, so Logz.io gives you a place to cap it.

Admins set the account's AI limits in **Settings > Plan and usage > AI Capping**. The tab shows the same capping panel as the OrionIQ Platform's **Capping** page, with the **Monthly Budget**, **Daily Invocation Limit**, and **Account Daily Token Limit**, and you edit it in place. Click **Save** to apply your changes.

See [OrionIQ Settings > Capping](/docs/user-guide/orioniq/settings/#capping) for what each limit covers, how soft limits work, and the fixed budget on trial and free accounts.

Links to capping from the OrionIQ chat drawer, such as the message an admin sees when the account reaches its limit, open this tab. The older **AI Settings** tab address opens it too.

Find these limits before you roll AI features out to a team, not after.

## If AI is disabled

AI features on your account, chat and agent invocations alike, are controlled by a single account-wide AI usage setting. When they are disabled, the AI Capping tab shows the notice "AI based features are disabled on this account. Contact support to enable them." Contact [Logz.io support](https://logz.io/support-page/) to enable them.
